How it works

No cables, no MIDI, no special piano. Any acoustic or digital piano works.

1

Stand the iPad or iPhone on the music stand

Clefwing listens through the device's microphone, right where the music would be.

2

Read the note, play the key

Short lessons of quick questions: name the note, play it, then play runs of notes and chords.

3

The dragon hears it

Right notes turn green straight away. A wrong note shows faintly on the staff, so your child sees how far off it was.

What your child learns

Thirteen units, with 100 lessons and 49 short mini-lessons, for beginners through to about Grade 3. It works well alongside AMEB and other grade-exam piano lessons.

  • Treble and bass clef notes, found from landmark notes like middle C
  • Both hands on the grand staff
  • Hand positions and finger numbers, so eyes can stay on the music
  • Steps, skips and leaps: reading the jump between notes
  • Rhythm: keeping the beat, note lengths, rests, time signatures, dotted notes and ties, played in time with a click
  • Reading ahead: notes that disappear as you play, look-and-play patterns, and a scrolling reader
  • Reading real pieces: Ode to Joy, a few bars at a time, sung first and then played, up to both hands, then a second tune, Lightly Row
  • Ledger lines above and below the staff
  • Semitones and tones, sharps, flats and naturals
  • Key signatures: G, F and D major
  • Major and minor chords, and why they sound happy or sad

A Daily Review brings back the notes they find hardest, and lessons get longer runs of notes as their reading gets faster.

Questions

Does it work with a digital piano or keyboard?

Yes. Clefwing listens through the device's microphone, so any acoustic piano, digital piano or keyboard works, with no cables. On a digital piano, play through its speakers, not headphones.

Who is it for?

Young pianists from their very first notes to about Grade 3. Read-aloud explains each question for younger players, and grown-ups can follow along. It works alongside lessons with a teacher, or on its own.

Where should the iPad or iPhone go?

On the music stand, close to the keys, with nothing covering the microphone. A quiet room helps: a TV or other music playing nearby can get in the way.

What if there's no piano nearby?

An on-screen keyboard works too, for practice away from the piano. It earns half the gems, so the real piano stays the best way to play.

Can brothers and sisters share it?

Yes. Each child gets their own player, dragon and progress on the same device, and one purchase covers them all. Family Sharing shares it with your family's other devices too.

Does it need an account or the internet?

No account, ever. Lessons work offline, and progress is saved on the device with a backup in your own iCloud. Nothing about your child is collected.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. The app is for iPad and iPhone, and the free units also play in a web browser on a computer or tablet with a microphone.

Privacy

Clefwing collects no personal information.

  • Progress stays on your device, with a backup in your own iCloud. It is never sent to us.
  • The microphone only listens for piano notes during lessons. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts and no chat.
  • Gems are earned by practising only and can't be bought. The full course is bought through Apple, behind a grown-ups' check.
