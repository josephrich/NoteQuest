Learn to read music by playing your real piano

Clefwing is a note-reading game for young pianists. A note appears on the staff, your child plays it on your piano, and their dragon hears it through the microphone. Every answer is a real key on a real piano, so reading notes becomes automatic.

How it works

No cables, no MIDI, no special piano. Any acoustic or digital piano works.

1

Stand the iPad or iPhone on the music stand

Clefwing listens through the device's microphone, right where the music would be.

2

Read the note, play the key

Short lessons of quick questions: name the note, play it, then play runs of notes and chords.

3

The dragon hears it

Right notes turn green straight away. A wrong note shows faintly on the staff, with "go higher" or "go lower".

What your child learns

Eight units, over 50 lessons and 20 short mini-lessons, for beginners through to about Grade 3. It works well alongside AMEB and other grade-exam piano lessons.

  • Treble and bass clef notes, found from landmark notes like middle C
  • Both hands on the grand staff, and ledger lines above and below
  • Steps, skips and leaps: reading the jump between notes
  • Semitones and tones, sharps, flats and naturals
  • Major and minor chords, and why they sound happy or sad
  • Key signatures: G, F and D major

A Daily Review brings back the notes they find hardest, and lessons get longer runs of notes as their reading gets faster.

For grown-ups

  • See practice time for every day, and how fast and accurately each note is read
  • Set a daily goal and a practice reminder
  • Add real-life prizes, like choosing Friday dinner, for gems to be spent on
  • A separate player for each child, each with their own dragon and progress
  • The Grown-ups area sits behind a quick times-table question
  • No piano nearby? An on-screen keyboard works too, for half the gems

Privacy

Clefwing collects no personal information.

  • Progress stays on your device. It is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • The microphone only listens for piano notes during lessons. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts and no chat.
  • Gems are earned by practising only. Nothing is sold for real money.
