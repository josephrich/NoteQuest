## Description (max 4000)

```
Clefwing turns piano note reading into a game kids want to play every day.

A note appears on the staff. Your child plays it on your real piano. Clefwing listens through the microphone and knows straight away if it was right, so every answer is a real key pressed on a real piano, not a guess on a screen. That's how note reading becomes automatic.

BUILT BY A PIANO FAMILY, FOR PIANO FAMILIES
Clefwing started as a way to help one 8-year-old with his reading. It teaches the way good piano teachers do: from landmark notes like middle C and the G line, with short explanations, lots of quick practice, and hints that show how to find a note rather than just giving the answer.

WHAT YOUR CHILD LEARNS
• Treble and bass clef notes, found from landmarks
• Both hands on the grand staff
• Ledger lines above and below the staff
• Steps, skips and leaps: reading the jump between notes
• Semitones and tones, sharps, flats and naturals
• Major and minor chords, and what makes them sound happy or sad
Seven units, with over 50 lessons and 20 short mini-lessons, suited to children around ages 6 to 12, and helpful alongside AMEB and other grade-exam piano lessons.

PRACTICE THAT ADAPTS
• A Daily Review mixes everything they know, focusing on the notes they find hardest
• As they get better, lessons bring in more runs of notes and longer melodies
• When they're stuck, a hint shows the nearest landmark on the staff so they can work it out
• Wrong notes show as a faint "ghost" on the staff, so they can see if they need to go higher or lower
• Chords are checked note by note: the right notes turn green

A GAME THEY'LL COME BACK TO
• Their own dragon to name, dress up and cheer them on
• XP, streaks, daily goals and treasure chests with gems to spend in the shop
• Bonus XP for improving and for practising lessons back to back, not just for being fast
• Grown-ups can add real-life prizes (a trip to the park, picking the family movie) for gems to be spent on

FOR THE WHOLE FAMILY
• A separate player for each child, each with their own dragon and progress
• A Grown-ups area (behind a quick maths question) with the daily goal, practice reminders, and which notes are tricky
• Works on iPad and iPhone, with or without an internet connection

NO PIANO NEARBY?
Practise on the on-screen keyboard instead (it earns half XP, because a real piano is the best way to learn).

SAFE AND PRIVATE
• No ads, no accounts, no chat, no in-app purchases
• No personal data collected: progress stays on your device
• The microphone only listens for piano notes while a lesson is running. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere

The read-aloud voice, which helps younger children follow the instructions, is AI-generated.

Questions or ideas? We'd love to hear from you: clefwingnotes@gmail.com
```

## App Review notes

```
Clefwing is a piano note-reading game for children. Lessons show a note and listen through the microphone for the child to play it on a real piano (audio is analysed on the device only; nothing is recorded or sent).

Testing without a piano: on the home screen, set "Playing on" to "Screen". Lessons then use an on-screen keyboard instead of the microphone. If the microphone permission is declined, lessons also fall back to the on-screen keyboard.

To try later units without completing earlier ones: tap the gear icon (Grown-ups) on the home screen, answer the times-table question, and turn on "Unlock every lesson".

There are no accounts, purchases, ads or links out of the app. Treasure chests contain virtual gems and dragon items earned by practising only; their odds are shown in the shop.

The read-aloud voice was pre-recorded with an AI text-to-speech service and is disclosed in the description.
```
