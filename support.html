Clefwing Help

Piano note reading for kids

Need a hand? Email clefwingnotes@gmail.com. We read every message and usually reply within a few days.

Clefwing can't hear my piano

  • Check Clefwing is allowed to use the microphone: Settings › Clefwing › Microphone.
  • Stand the iPad or iPhone on the piano's music stand, close to the keys, with nothing covering the microphone.
  • Turn off a sustain pedal while you play, and keep the room quiet (TV and talking can confuse it).
  • In Grown-ups (tap ⚙︎ on the home screen), use the tuning check: if your piano is a little out of tune, Clefwing adjusts to it.
  • Digital pianos work too. Turn the volume up, and play through the speakers rather than headphones.

We don't have a piano with us

On the home screen, set Playing on to 📱 Screen to play on an on-screen keyboard instead. It earns half XP: a real piano is the best way to learn, because your fingers learn where the notes are.

More than one child in the family

Each child can have their own player, with their own dragon, progress and gems. Tap your name at the top of the home screen, then Add a player. Grown-ups can remove players in the Grown-ups area.

Will we lose our progress?

Progress is saved on your device only (Clefwing has no accounts and sends nothing anywhere). It's included in your device's normal iCloud or computer backup. Deleting the app deletes its progress.

Grown-ups area

Tap ⚙︎ on the home screen and answer a quick times-table question. There you can set the daily goal and reminders, add real-life prizes to spend gems on, see which notes are tricky, check the microphone and tuning, and manage players.

Is it safe for kids?

Yes: no ads, no accounts, no chat, no in-app purchases, and no personal data collected. Gems are earned by practising only. See the privacy policy.

About the voice

The read-aloud voice is AI-generated (recorded in advance with OpenAI text-to-speech). Nothing you say or play is recorded or sent anywhere.
